# Incident Response Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines how security incidents are detected, triaged, contained, and reported.

## Incident Classification
Incidents are classified as Critical, High, Medium, or Low severity. Critical incidents include confirmed data exfiltration, ransomware detonation, and loss of control of production infrastructure.

## Notification Requirements
The security incident response team must be notified within 1 hour of detecting a suspected Critical incident. For confirmed Critical incidents, the Chief Information Security Officer and legal counsel must be notified within 24 hours. Affected customers must be notified within 72 hours where required by law or contract.

## Containment
Critical incidents require containment actions to begin within 4 hours of confirmation. Forensic evidence must be preserved before any affected system is rebuilt or returned to service.

## Post-Incident Review
A post-incident review is required for every Critical and High severity incident within 10 business days of resolution. The review must document root cause, timeline, and corrective actions with assigned owners.
