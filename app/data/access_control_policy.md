# Access Control Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines how user access to corporate systems is granted, reviewed, and revoked.

## Password Requirements
All user passwords must be at least 14 characters long and include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, digits, and symbols. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days. The previous 12 passwords may not be reused.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access. Administrative accounts must use hardware-based MFA tokens. Biometric factors may be used as a second factor but never as the sole factor.

## Access Reviews
Access rights for privileged accounts are reviewed quarterly by the security team. Standard user access is reviewed annually. Departing employees have all access revoked within 4 hours of termination.

## Least Privilege
Users are granted the minimum access required for their job function. Privileged access requires documented business justification and manager approval.
