# Data Retention Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines how long categories of corporate data are retained and how they are disposed of.

## Retention Periods
Financial records, including invoices, ledgers, and tax filings, are retained for 7 years. Employee personnel records are retained for 5 years after employment ends. System security logs are retained for 13 months. Email is retained for 3 years unless subject to a legal hold.

## Legal Holds
When litigation is reasonably anticipated, the legal team places a legal hold that suspends normal deletion schedules for all relevant data. Holds remain in effect until released in writing by legal counsel.

## Disposal
Data that reaches the end of its retention period is destroyed using approved methods. Paper records are cross-cut shredded. Electronic media is cryptographically erased or physically destroyed. Certificates of destruction are retained for all third-party disposal.

## Exceptions
Requests to retain data beyond its scheduled period require written approval from the data owner and legal counsel, and must be reviewed annually.
